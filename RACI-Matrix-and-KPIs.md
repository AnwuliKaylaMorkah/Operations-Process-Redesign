# Governance (RACI) & Operational KPI Impact

## 1. Cross-Functional RACI Matrix
* **R = Responsible** (Executes the task)
* **A = Accountable** (Final ownership/sign-off — one per task)
* **C = Consulted** (Provides input/resolution)
* **I = Informed** (Kept updated on status)

| Process Stage | AP Analyst | Procurement Buyer | Warehouse / Dept Lead | Finance Manager |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **1. PO Creation & Approval** | I | **R** | **A** | C |
| **2. Goods Receipt (GRN) Logging** | I | I | **A / R** | I |
| **3. OCR Intake & Duplicate Check** | **A / R** | I | I | I |
| **4. 3-Way Match Exception Resolution** | **R** | C | C | **A** |
| **5. Non-PO Invoice Approval (>€500)** | C | I | **R** | **A** |
| **6. Final Payment Batch Release** | C | I | I | **A / R** |

## 2. Operational & Financial KPI Improvements
*(Baseline modelled on a mid-sized firm processing 1,500 invoices/month with €1.2M monthly spend)*

| KPI Metric | Before Redesign | After Redesign | Operational & Financial Impact |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **End-to-End Cycle Time** | 14.5 Days | 5.0 Days | **-65.5%** faster turnaround |
| **Straight-Through Processing (STP)** | 15% | 72% | **+57% pts** automated without human touch |
| **Invoice Exception / Rework Rate** | 12.0% | 3.5% | **-70.8%** reduction in manual dispute resolution |
| **Cost Per Invoice Processed** | €11.50 | €3.80 | **€11,550/month** saved in administrative overhead |
| **Early Payment Discount Capture** | 10% | 85% | **€48,000+/year** direct bottom-line savings |
